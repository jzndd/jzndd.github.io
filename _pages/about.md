<div class="about-intro" markdown="1">

I am **Zhennan Jiang (江震南)**, a Ph.D. student in Computer Applications Technology at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences ([CASIA](https://ia.cas.cn/)). I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Dongbin Zhao](https://ia.cas.cn/rcdw/yjy/202404/t20240422_7129930.html) (IEEE Fellow) and [Dr. Haoran Li](https://ia.cas.cn/rcdw/fyjy/202404/t20240422_7129926.html).

I received my Bachelor's degree in Automation from Central South University. I am also a joint-training student at Zhongguancun Academy ([ZGCA](https://www.bza.edu.cn/en)), working with [Prof. Chao Yu](https://nicsefc.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/people/ChaoYu).

My research focuses on building **reliable, efficient, and generalizable learning systems for real-world robots**, with particular interests in reinforcement learning, generative policies, and world models.
